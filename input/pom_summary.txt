### Available Page Objects
- `HomePage(page)`: Represents https://automationintesting.online/. Methods: `navigate()`, `fill_contact_form(name, email, phone, subject, message)`, `submit_contact_form()`, `click_book_single_room()`.
- `AdminPage(page)`: Represents https://automationintesting.online/admin. Methods: `navigate()`, `login(username, password)`, `logout()`.
- `ReservationPage(page, room_id)`: Represents /reservation/{room_id}. Methods: `navigate()`.
- `CookiePage(page)`: Represents /cookie. Methods: `navigate()`.
- `PrivacyPage(page)`: Represents /privacy. Methods: `navigate()`.
